Why B2 vs B1/B3 (per RESEARCH § D-RF-03 Recommendation):
- **Not B1 (status quo)**: the current `"norm"` substring heuristic *does*
  fire on every RF-DETR LayerNorm node (vendor PyTorch export names every
  norm op `.../norm1/LayerNormalization`, `.../norm2/LayerNormalization`,
  etc. — all match "norm"). But that's coincidence — driven by PyTorch's
  auto-naming convention. A future model (Phase 10 D-FINE / DEIMv2, or a
  vendor with a custom node-naming scheme) could silently miss its LayerNorm
  layers. The explicit `LayerType.NORMALIZATION` clause hardens the contract
  at near-zero cost.
- **Not B3 (subgraph pattern matcher)**: the decomposed-LayerNorm pitfall
  (ReduceMean -> Sub -> Pow -> ... -> Add) does NOT manifest on RF-DETR at
  opset 18 (Plan 08-02 checkpoint verified: 0 ReduceMean, 0 Pow in the
  simplified graph; 51 single-node LayerNormalization preserved). Building
  and maintaining a subgraph matcher for a case that doesn't occur would be
  wasted code that Phase 10 would also inherit.
- **B2 wins** because it costs ~3 lines, is reusable verbatim in Phase 10
  (the Phase-10-inheritance note in RESEARCH § D-RF-03 explicitly carries
  the patch forward), and documents intent: we want to FP16 the
  *normalization layers* specifically, not whatever substring "norm"
  happens to match.
